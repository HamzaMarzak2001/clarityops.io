FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a typical project take?

Most builds run 4 to 5 weeks from kickoff to handoff. Simpler workspaces can be ready sooner; larger systems with multiple automations and integrations take a little longer. We give you a clear timeline on the discovery call.

What if I already have a Notion workspace?

That's common. We'll audit what you have, keep what's working, and rebuild or restructure the rest. You don't lose your data; we migrate everything into a system that actually scales.

Do I need to know Notion to work with you?

Not at all. Most of our clients aren't Notion experts, which is exactly why they hire us. We handle the build and train your team so the system is easy to use from day one.

How much does a typical project cost?

Most projects range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on complexity. A simple Notion workspace setup sits at the lower end. A full system with multiple automations, AI integrations, and team training sits higher. We scope everything on the discovery call, with no surprises.

What industries do you work with?

We've built systems for construction companies, creative agencies, media firms, real estate operations, e-commerce brands, SaaS startups, and more. The common thread isn't industry. It's teams that need better systems.

Do you offer ongoing support after the project?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer retainer partnerships for teams that want a dedicated systems partner for ongoing optimization, new builds, and priority support.

How is working with ClarityOps different from buying a Notion template?

Templates assume a generic workflow. The moment your process differs (and it always does), you're either changing how you work or hacking the template. We build from scratch around how your team actually operates. Plus, we handle the automations, AI integrations, and team training that no template can provide.

Can I see examples of your work?

Absolutely. Our case studies section shows real projects with real results. On the discovery call, we can also walk you through relevant examples from similar industries or use cases.
