Before you book.

What does this cost?
Every build is quoted as one fixed price, and you see that number in your Blueprint before you commit to the build, not as an hourly meter that moves. What drives it: how many databases, how many automations, how many platforms, how many people we train, and how many records we migrate. We give you a real range on the 30-minute call, so you find out in half an hour rather than three weeks in.
What if my team doesn’t actually use it?
That’s the real risk in this kind of work, and it’s why the last phase is called Embed rather than Handover. You get video documentation, a written SOP library and a live training session. Then: every manual step marked “eliminate” on your signed ledger is gone at handover, or we keep building, training and simplifying at no extra charge until it is, for up to four more weeks.
Is this just a Notion setup?
No. Notion is where the work lives. The automations are what make it move, and the AI agent is what does work inside it. For MAE Media that meant 10+ automations across 8 platforms, including QuickBooks cash and receivables feeding a live scorecard and a daily Meta Ads sync across two ad accounts. That isn’t a workspace; it’s an operating layer.
What happens to all our existing data?
It moves. Migration is included, up to 650 records. We migrated 629 heir records into Bourbon Holdings’ deal desk. You don’t do the copying and you don’t pay extra for it.
I already have a Notion workspace. Is that wasted?
No. The Blueprint audits what you already have, keeps what works, and the architecture you approve shows exactly what gets rebuilt versus retained. Nobody pays us to throw away work they already did.
How much of my own time does this take?
About 4 hours across five weeks: one 90-minute session, two 45-minute reviews, one 60-minute training. Plus feedback inside 48 hours at each milestone. We do the migration and write the documentation.
What if the price moves or the timeline slips?
The price quoted in your Blueprint is the price you pay; scope additions are quoted in writing and approved by you before they’re built. Live by day 35, or any additional week that’s our fault is free, provided we had tool access by day 2 and feedback inside 48 hours.
Why not just buy a template, or hire someone in-house?
A template gives you someone else’s structure and the job of filling it in. An in-house hire gets you all three layers eventually, if they stay, and costs more than this in the first quarter. If a template genuinely is the right answer for you, we’ll say so.
Do I need to be technical?
No. You need to know how your business works. We handle everything else, and your team gets trained on the finished system with a Loom for each part of it.
What happens after the 30 days?
Nothing, unless you want it to. You own every database, every automation and every agent: no licence, no lock-in, no recurring fee. If you want us to keep building, there’s an optional monthly retainer you can cancel any time, and everything keeps running when you do.
